Mouse cerebellar Purkinje layer — phenotype composition by lobule
=================================================================

What you see:  The Purkinje cell layer of the mouse cerebellum in 3D,
               rotating 180°. Each lobule lights up in turn, colored by
               the Purkinje subtypes it contains. The panel lists that
               lobule's composition.

Geometry:      2,279,886 voxels at 10 µm, computed as the boundary
               between the granular and molecular layers (the released
               CCFv3a annotation volume contains no Purkinje-layer voxels,
               so the layer is derived geometrically).
Phenotypes:    16,634 Purkinje cells, 9 subtypes, 16 lobules.

Made with:     Python (NumPy, Pillow) + ffmpeg. 1920×1080, 60 fps, 40 s.
               Custom software rasterizer — no GPU, no game engine.
